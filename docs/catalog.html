Space-Time Crystal catalog

All 275 space-time groups, each one animated. The classification is S. Xu & C. Wu, Space-Time Crystal and Space-Time Group, Phys. Rev. Lett. 120, 096401 (2018), arXiv:1703.03388, whose appendix C carries the 2+1D count, tabulated by system in Ke & Wu (2026) and recomputed independently here. Groups are numbered xu1…xu275, the same numbering the colour-correspondence tables use. Click a card to open a group's own page.

Cards start paused: ▶ below a card plays it in place, and pointing at a card lets ← → jump between the loop's symmetry instants (shift: step frame by frame). The selection is in the address bar, so a filtered catalog can be linked to.
