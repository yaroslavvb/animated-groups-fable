Do not subtract the first row from the third

Wieting counts every transitive action on N colours, while a single clock only supplies a cyclic regular action. Plane colourings and film groups are then quotiented by different affine equivalences. The columns answer related questions, not nested versions of one question.

2. Which colourings become clocks?

Let Γ be a wallpaper group and H the subgroup preserving one chosen colour. A perfect N-colouring is a transitive coset action on Γ/H, so [Γ:H] = N. It extends directly to one forward film clock precisely when

H ◁ Γ  and  Γ/H ≅ CN,

or equivalently when there is a surjection χ:Γ ↠ CN with kernel H. Normalize a film period to one and embed CN in ℝ/ℤ by ι(j̅) = j/N. The corresponding graph extension is

Gχ = {(γ, t) ∈ Γ × ℝ : t mod ℤ = ι(χ(γ))}.

The colour assigned by χ has become a time offset. Conversely, a forward film with a fixed spatial projection and finite phase image produces such a cyclic character. For N = 2 every index-two subgroup is normal with quotient C2, so the independently derived cyclic census reproduces Wieting's classical 46 exactly.

Projection is not a single frame

The catalog places its 275 canonical representatives into 17 wallpaper projection categories: forget each operation's time component and retain its spatial action. This is not, in general, the symmetry group visible in one frame. For a forward film, the symmetries preserving a generic frame form the zero-phase kernel H, often a proper subgroup of the projected wallpaper group Γ. Reversing groups can also have special-frame stabilizers; accidental motif symmetries are a separate issue.

3. Why the cyclic and film rows differ

The catalog uses the 275-class spacetime equivalence: Galilean boosts and spacetime-lattice re-basings are allowed, and proper three-dimensional orientation is retained. A plane colour class therefore need not remain one film normal form of the same displayed order.

Why five disappears

There are four regular cyclic five-colour plane types, but planar crystallographic point groups have no order-five operation, so their C5 phase is carried by translations. Under the broader film re-basing equivalence, their corresponding classes have canonical representatives in lower-order clock bins. Hence the canonical order-five film bin is zero.

Why one class can split

The wallpaper group p3 has two regular-cyclic three-colour types. Its gyrational kernel becomes the chiral pair 313131 and 323232; its sublattice kernel becomes r33132. Two unoriented colour kernels therefore give three oriented film normal forms.

These examples explain mechanisms, not a canonical subtraction between the aggregate rows. A spacetime re-basing can change the chosen spatial projection and clock order, so this report does not claim a class-by-class correspondence where the two equivalence problems do not supply one.

5. Data and reproduction

Download the generated JSON census, summary CSV, or wallpaper-by-wallpaper CSV. The generator recovers the small rational time offsets, assigns every forward entry its least common denominator, and checks all totals and row sums:

python3 enumerate/color_forward_census.py
python3 enumerate/color_forward_census.py --check
node enumerate/verify_cyclic_colors.mjs ../wieting-subgroups

The Node verification command uses the pinned Senechal–Wieting subgroup engine: it retains normal index-N subgroups with cyclic quotient, takes affine-normalizer orbits, checks all 17 × 6 cells, and asserts that the result is stable for matrix-entry bounds one through six. The N = 2 total 46 is an external calibration because every two-colour stabilizer is automatically regular cyclic.

6. Scope boundary

This report counts how classical plane colour data is read as the clock displayed by a canonical forward representative. It does not count independent N-colourings of each of the 68 forward film groups. That would be a new three-dimensional finite-index subgroup census and would first require a choice among parent-group, full affine, and Galilean equivalence. No such larger number is asserted here.
