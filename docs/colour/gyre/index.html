Entangled colourings · colouring 1 of 2

Gyre turn, wait, recolour

Three colours welded to a third-turn about a point of no symmetry. Turn the picture a third of a turn about p, run the film on a third of a period and step every colour back one, and nothing changes; do any two of the three and it does. A cyclic colour group is allowed to be fully entangled — nothing at all surviving in a single frozen frame — and that is the character this catalog was selected for; the counts below say how many of the admitted entries actually have it, and every entry says for itself, in its badges and in its own measured table. Every entry is a saved orbit of the named equation, carrying its own verification and a colour law re-derived from its own bytes at every node-frame.

1 · The base film group

The colour law is a theorem for any field on a triangular lattice and any turn centre, so every hexagonal film group can host it. What differs is what the picture then looks like, whether the argmax ever ties — where it does, the law fails at exactly those nodes and the field is refused — and whether any extra symmetry leaks into the colour-preserving subgroup.

Why some fields cannot host this colouring —

    Every refusal is logged with its reason in colour-atlas-build.json, beside the catalog. A tie in the argmax is decided by index order, and the relabelling does not carry index order along, so the colour law fails at exactly the tied nodes: the builder measures the law rather than assuming it, and admits only an exact 1.
