How the diagram is constructed

Each atlas record lists its operations as a planar matrix, planar displacement and clock-axis shift. We compare these operations modulo integer translations in all three directions. An inclusion is drawn when every operation in the upper representative is also in the lower one, and no third displayed representative lies strictly between them. The vertical arrangement follows these inclusions; horizontal position has no mathematical meaning.

Space groups also have subgroups with different translation lattices, origin choices and embeddings. Those are outside this fixed-setting diagram. For the broader crystallographic subgroup problem, see the Bilbao Crystallographic Server’s maximal-subgroup tool.
