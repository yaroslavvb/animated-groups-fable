Those runs are folded, two ways: every card of one wave — the same name under the same film group and equation, whatever its parameters or the rule it was read by — folds behind one card, and so does every clip that measures alike frame by frame. A single card is shown carrying a +6 alike chip that opens the rest in place, right behind it. What you can check by opening a chip: every card behind it is the same wave as the one shown or looks like it, and no two cards a group shows measure alike. Nothing is dropped, either: every folded card keeps its own parameters, its own reading and its own link, and Fold look-alikes in the bar below turns the whole thing off. Only the plain-threshold controls are left out: they carry no two-colour law at all, and they are read beside their own picture in the monochrome explorer, where the page can say so.
