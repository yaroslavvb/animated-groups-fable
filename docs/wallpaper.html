The wallpaper atlas

The 17 wallpaper groups, and over each one every film group whose spatial projection it is — the whole catalog, one wallpaper at a time.

Every film group projects to a wallpaper group: forget the clock, keep every spatial operation. This is the symmetry of the long-exposure photograph, and it is the base symbol of the clockwork orbifold notation (for reversal groups, including any bracketed enrichment). Each section below describes what happens over one wallpaper group, shows one or two characteristic examples, and links to a page with all of its film groups at full size. The section counts sum to 275; captions link to the catalog for generators and details.
