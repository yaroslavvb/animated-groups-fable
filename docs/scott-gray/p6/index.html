EUCLIDEAN SPACE / CYCLIC TIME

A triangular lattice, with the correct timing.

The sixfold family uses lattice vectors (1, 0) and (−½, √3/2). The viewer draws them at their actual angles and lengths. Each rotation is combined with its assigned fraction of the period; its short color symbol encodes that cyclic action. A nonzero phase shift must make a measurable difference from the same spatial rotation at the original time.

The saved fields pass independent numerical checks for periodic return, time symmetry, motion and spatial structure. These finite-grid tests do not constitute a mathematical proof of a continuum orbit. No failed candidate is displayed as a solution, and an empty parameter list does not prove nonexistence.
