The moves
  • Edge flip. A cuff between two pairs of pants has four neighbours; the flip pairs them the next way round, . Of the curves that pair them so and cross the old cuff twice, the shortest is taken; if it is longer than the page can draw and the pairing after it is not, that one is taken. An arc between two mirror polygons flips to the common perpendicular of the other two mirrors.
  • Handle swap. A cuff with both sides on one pair of pants is replaced by the shortest loop that crosses it once.
  • Mirror pop and merge. A cuff with a mirror behind it opens into arcs that run from the mirror round each of the two things in front of it, trading its Twist for a second Length; two such arcs close into the cuff again.
  • Slide. An arc that runs from a mirror round a cone point or a cuff, next to a mirror polygon, moves to the polygon's third mirror.

A move changes coordinates, not the pattern: the group stays as it is, the new Length is read off the trace of the new curve's motion, and each Twist changes by the step the foot of a seam takes along its cuff.

The cuts are not drawn: a cut is shown by the special points it parts, one side filled in its colour and the other ringed in it, while it is pointed at. A cut that parts no special points (a handle's loop, a crosscap, a mirror) is drawn as its line. Tick cuts to see every cut and the seams of the pieces, and to flip a cut by a click on the pattern.

Undo steps back through flips and slider moves (also ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z). The address of the page holds the cut and its numbers, so a link brings a state back.

How many numbers

On the hyperbolic plane an orbifold with underlying surface , cone points and mirror corners has numbers of shape. A cuff between two pieces carries two of them (Length, Twist); a crosscap, a smooth mirror and an arc carry one.

An orbifold with none is rigid: every type on the sphere, eight on the plane, and , , on the hyperbolic plane. It is drawn with the tile and the print of Classification of Surfaces, and has nothing to cut. On the plane the area is fixed, which leaves nine types with one or two numbers. Two features, two handles works four small hyperbolic types by hand.

What is not built
  • Two crosscaps cannot yet be traded for a one-sided handle (a fourth move); every crosscap stays cut along its own core.
  • A cuff between two mirrors with corners, or between such a mirror and a crosscap, offers no pop.
  • A move that would make a cuff longer than 9.5 or an arc longer than 6 is turned down: such a piece is a sliver on the screen.
  • A Length stops when a piece would get thinner than 0.6 of what it starts as (the distance from its middle to its nearest side, at the numbers its decomposition has at first: every cuff , every arc ): past that the commas, which keep their size, run into each other.
  • A comma is never changed, only moved: each is tied to a corner or to the middle of a piece, a Twist carries whole pieces past each other, and a flip leaves every comma where it is. So commas of different pieces can come to lie over each other after a flip or when a piece gets small. A link opens the same orbifold cut the same way with the same numbers, with the print laid afresh.
